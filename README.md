## About me

I am a **Software and Machine Learning Engineer at Coldsense Technologies** and an MSc Computer Science and Engineering student at **Politecnico di Milano**.

I build end-to-end AI systems—from product interfaces, APIs and data pipelines to retrieval, model evaluation, inference and deployment.

My focus is on:

- Agentic workflows with tools, state, validation and retries
- RAG with hybrid retrieval, reranking and grounded generation
- Fine-tuning and self-hosting open-source models
- Integrating frontier models where their reasoning justifies the cost
- Shipping reliable AI products with Docker, CI/CD and cloud infrastructure
